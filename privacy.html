Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Sniftr ("the app") does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Everything you enter in Sniftr — tasting notes, scores, brands, and any other information — is stored locally on your device only. The app does not connect to the internet, does not use analytics or advertising services, and does not send any information to the developer or any third party.

Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, sell, or disclose. If you delete the app, all of its data is removed with it.

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/tjhughes001a/sniftr-site/issues.
